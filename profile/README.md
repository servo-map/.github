# Servo Map

Fuel prices for Australia, from the official source. Servo Map reads each state's fuel price
reporting scheme and shows every servo's price at [www.servo-map.com](https://www.servo-map.com),
with an app for iPhone and CarPlay that is coming to the App Store.

- **Coverage:** New South Wales, Queensland, Western Australia, South Australia, Tasmania and the
  Australian Capital Territory. Victoria and the Northern Territory are not covered yet.
- **Prices:** in cents per litre, each with the time the servo reported it. Nothing is estimated: a
  servo that has not reported shows no price.
- **On the website:** a page for every suburb and servo, each state's price trend and cycle, in
  eight languages. [How the data is sourced](https://www.servo-map.com/about).

Servo Map is an independent project, not affiliated with any government agency or fuel retailer.
